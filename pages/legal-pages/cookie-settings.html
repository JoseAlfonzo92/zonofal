Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

Zonofal does not use cookies or tracking technologies for analytics, advertising, or user profiling. We respect your privacy and ensure that your browsing experience on our site is free from tracking-based technologies.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites you visit. They are commonly used to enable functionality, analyze usage, or support advertising. Zonofal does not use cookies for any of these purposes.

2. Our Approach to Privacy

We follow a privacy-first approach. This means we avoid unnecessary data collection, do not track visitors, do not profile users, and do not sell, rent, or monetize personal data. Any information you provide (such as through contact forms) is used solely to respond to your request.

3. Third-Party Services

Zonofal uses a limited number of carefully selected third-party services to provide essential website functionality, security, and media delivery. These services are not used by Zonofal for advertising, behavioral profiling, or analytics. Depending on how you interact with the website, providers such as Google Fonts, YouTube, Cloudflare Turnstile, or similar services may process limited technical information (such as your IP address, browser details, or device information) as necessary to deliver their functionality. If you access external websites through links on our site, those providers may use their own cookies, tracking technologies, or privacy practices. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party services you use.

4. Third-Party Providers

To provide a secure, reliable, and modern browsing experience, Zonofal may rely on carefully selected third-party providers. These services are used solely to support website functionality, media delivery, typography, accessibility, and security, and are not used by Zonofal for advertising, behavioral profiling, or analytics. Examples include Google Fonts for web typography, YouTube for embedded media content, and Cloudflare Turnstile for spam prevention and security verification. These providers may process limited technical information, such as IP addresses, browser details, or device information, only to the extent necessary to provide their respective services. Their processing of personal information is governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

4. Essential Storage

Zonofal may use minimal browser-based storage technologies such as localStorage or sessionStorage strictly for essential functionality, including remembering language, currency, accessibility, and other user interface preferences. This storage is not used for analytics, advertising, tracking, profiling, or cross-site identification, and does not monitor user behavior across websites, sessions, or devices.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to browse Zonofal without being tracked. You also have the right to request deletion of any personal information you provide through forms or communications, in accordance with our Privacy Policy.

6. Consent

Zonofal does not use cookies or browser storage for advertising, behavioral profiling, or analytics. Any browser storage used by Zonofal is limited to essential website functionality and user preferences. Certain third-party services integrated into the website, such as media providers or security tools, may use their own technologies as necessary to provide their functionality. Where required by applicable law, user consent will be requested before non-essential technologies are used. The processing of data by third-party providers is governed by their respective privacy policies.

7. Client Websites

Websites developed or delivered by Zonofal are provided to clients under a license agreement, as described in our Terms of Service. Once delivered, these websites are operated and managed by the client in accordance with that license. Depending on the client's requirements, a website may use cookies, browser storage, analytics platforms, payment providers, APIs, embedded content, content delivery networks (CDNs), or other third-party services. Zonofal does not control, manage, or assume responsibility for the privacy practices, cookie usage, availability, or policies of client-operated websites or their third-party providers after deployment. Clients are responsible for complying with applicable privacy and data protection laws and for providing their own Cookie Policy and Privacy Policy where required.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Cookie Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us at hello@zonofal.com

Zonofal is built with privacy and transparency in mind. Your information is only used when you choose to contact us. We never sell your personal data.
